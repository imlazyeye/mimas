# LLM Usage Policy

This is the policy for LLM usage in any and all contributions towards `mimas`. It applies to code, documentation, issues, pull requests, and discussion. For any questions or concerns, please contact the maintainers directly at `imlazyeye@gmail.com`.

## Overview

Usage of LLMs while working on `mimas` is conditionally allowed. However, `mimas` is a project designed and written by humans, and an LLM should be treated as nothing more than a tool that assists you in your own authorship. It is not a co-author, and it is not a substitute for understanding.

These are the general principles you should follow when using an LLM on this project:

* **Discovery is okay.** It is acceptable to use LLMs as a tool for researching new concepts, reviewing your work, diagnosing bugs, and exploring approaches you then discard. Generally speaking, use of LLMs that exists only within your own chat is your prerogative.
* **You make every judgement call.** An LLM may propose, but only you may decide. The ultimate design and approach of your contributions should be the product of your own knowledge and taste. If you find yourself accepting a design because the model suggested it rather than because you requested (or extensively reviewed) it, stop and go back.
* **You must fully understand what you submit.** As a rule of thumb, if LLMs were to suddenly vanish, you should still be completely equipped to explain, maintain, and recreate everything you submit.
* **LLMs must not supplant skills or knowledge you do not have.** Using one to move more efficiently through work you could do yourself is fine. Using one to produce work you could not have produced alone is not.
* **Human communication only.** Documentation, commit messages, issues, and pull requests should all be written by hand.

## Provenance

You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to contribute what you submit under this project's license and that it does not infringe anyone else's rights. This is already true of code you write by hand, and LLM output does not change it. If you have reason to think a generated passage reproduces someone else's work, do not submit it.

## Disclosure

So long as you follow the above rules you are not required to call out your specific LLM usage. With that in mind, it is still good practice to note any places it played a role so that the reviewers are able to analyze it with increased scrutiny. If you feel like your usage is on the borderline of our policy, just mention it in your pull request -- the maintainers will let you know if its acceptable or if changes need to be made before your contribution will be considered.

## Enforcement

Enforcement is the responsibility of the maintainers alone, and contributions they deem to be in violation will be rejected. Please do not attempt to identify or police LLM usage yourself. You are encouraged to reach out to the maintainers if you have any concerns.

## Future

This policy may change as the rapidly evolving landscape of LLMs and their usage in programming shifts. Our priority will always be to maximize the quality of `mimas` and to uplift both those who use it and those who contribute towards it.
